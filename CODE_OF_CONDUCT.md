# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

LinguaRelay welcomes users and contributors across languages, backgrounds, and
experience levels. Be respectful, assume good intent, discuss technical trade-offs
with evidence, and make room for people to ask basic questions.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
sexualized content, deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private
information, or pressuring someone to share recordings or data they cannot safely
disclose. Fabricated benchmark results, hidden model-license violations, and
knowingly unsafe advice are also incompatible with this project.

## Privacy in project spaces

Do not post API keys, private recordings, unredacted caption history, personal
identifiers, or sensitive device/account details. Maintainers may remove such
material immediately to protect the reporter and other participants.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this code and may warn,
temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants when necessary. Decisions
should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and focused on
keeping the project safe and constructive.

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately through a GitHub security
advisory and state that the report concerns community conduct. Immediate threats
should be reported to the appropriate local service rather than handled in a
public issue.

This code applies to project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions,
and public spaces where someone represents LinguaRelay.
